Academic Enrollment Terms of Service

Clear, transparent standards governing student enrollment, batch allocations, fee payments, and academic honor codes.

1. Academic Enrollment & Batch Cap

Enrollment at BrightPath Academy is confirmed upon completion of the registration procedure, grade assessment review, and payment of the applicable tuition installment. To maintain our high-touch academic mentorship, batches are strictly capped at 12 students for classroom programs and 15 students for online interactive tracks.

2. Free Demo Class Policy

Every newly registered student is eligible for one complimentary demo class per subject. Attending a free demo session implies no binding financial commitment. Advance seat reservations are valid for 48 hours following the demo.

3. Tuition Fee Schedules & Payments

Tuition fees may be paid on a Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual basis as selected during registration. Official computer-generated receipts are automatically generated and archived in the Parent Dashboard. Sibling discounts (10%) and academic merit scholarships apply automatically to eligible installments.

4. Attendance, Leave, & Makeup Classes

Consistent attendance is critical for AP exam readiness. In case of illness or planned family absence, parents must submit an advance notice via the Parent Portal “Report Absence” function. Where possible, recorded lecture links or weekend doubt-clearing sessions will be scheduled.

5. Academic Code of Conduct

BrightPath Academy fosters an encouraging, respectful, and focused learning environment. Disruptive behavior, academic dishonesty on mock examinations, or harassment of peers will result in formal counseling and potential withdrawal from coaching without refund.
