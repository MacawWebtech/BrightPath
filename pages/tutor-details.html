Faculty Biography

Pedagogy Philosophy & Academic Leadership

Empowering students to transition from formula memorization to profound analytical mastery.

Dr. Sarah Jenkins firmly believes that mathematical anxiety stems not from inherent inability, but from procedural instruction stripped of conceptual context. Over her 14-year career as a senior educator and academic coach, she has pioneered a structural pedagogy that deconstructs abstract calculus, geometry, and algebraic models into visual, intuitive first principles. By connecting theoretical proofs to geometric diagrams and practical algorithmic logic, Dr. Sharma ensures students understand not just how a formula functions, but why it is true. This foundational depth transforms mathematics from a subject of intimidation into an empowering, intellectual adventure.

Under Dr. Jenkins’s leadership as Head of the Mathematics Department at BrightPath Academy, her cohorts have achieved an unprecedented 98.4% distinction rate across AP & Honors and IB & College Prep AP exams, with over 140 students recording perfect 100/100 centum scores in Class 10 and Class 12. Beyond standardized exams, she directs BrightPath Academy’s Regional and National Olympiad coaching streams, where multiple mentees have qualified for RMO and INMO rounds. Her rigorous methodology pairs diagnostic gap-identification with systematic problem-solving schema drills, ensuring every learner develops the speed, precision, and confidence required to excel at the highest scholastic levels.

Proven Pedagogical Framework

Dr. Sarah Jenkins’s 4-Step Teaching Methodology

A systematic, scientifically structured approach engineered for conceptual durability and examination centum performance.

1

Concept Clarity & First Principles

Every theorem and mathematical identity is derived from axiomatic first principles using dynamic visual models, establishing intuitive roots before any formulaic computation begins.

2

Problem-Solving Patterns & Schema

Students classify complex questions into recurring structural archetypes. Schema recognition trains students to immediately identify optimal resolution pathways without hesitation.

3

Mock Test Training & Simulations

Bi-weekly invigilated test sessions mirror exact AP & Honors and IB & College Prep board time allocations and question typography, building psychological stamina and eliminating exam-day anxiety.

4

AP Exam Strategies & Step-Marking

Mastering examiner marking schemes, step-by-step layout standards, working notes, and unit specifications ensures that no careless oversights compromise a perfect centum score.

Continuous Diagnostic Error Logging
Each student maintains an individualized digital error ledger to isolate recurring procedural slips and reinforce weak topics before board mocks.
