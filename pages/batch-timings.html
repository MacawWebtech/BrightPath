Academic Excellence Philosophy

Why Our Batch Structure Succeeds

Every batch at BrightPath Academy is engineered around small cohort dynamics, diagnostic milestone tracking, and dual-mode flexibility.

Strict Small Batch Caps

With a strict ceiling of 10 to 12 students per class, tutors identify conceptual roadblocks immediately. No child sits unnoticed in the back row.

1:10 Max Ratio

Weekly Diagnostic Audits

Every Friday features a 30-minute benchmark assessment. Detailed score analytics are instantly published to the Parent Portal dashboard.

Real-Time Analytics

Seamless Dual Mode

Traveling or unwell? Easily switch to live HD classroom streaming with two clicks. All classroom lectures are archived in 1080p with time-stamped notes.

In-Center + Online
